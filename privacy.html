Vaha — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

This policy explains what information the Vaha dhikr app ("the App") handles and how. The App is published by YG Software ("we"). Contact: destekekibi4406@gmail.com

Summary

  • You don't need an account. We don't ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your dhikr counts, daily wird, goals and settings are stored only on your phone. We have no server and never see this data.
  • The App doesn't use your location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos.
  • The App shows Google AdMob ads. The ad service may process the device information described below.
  • Donations are paid through Google Play or the Apple App Store. Your card details never reach us.

1. Information stored on your phone

To work, the App saves the following in your phone's own storage: dhikr counts, your daily wird list, dates of completed dhikr, streak information, goals, reminder times, sound and vibration settings, and the plants in your "Vaham" garden. This information is not sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the App deletes it.

2. Backups

"Create backup" in Settings saves your data as a file and opens your phone's share sheet. You choose where the file goes (for example Google Drive, email or a message). The file is never sent to us. "Restore backup" reads the file you pick only on your phone.

If your phone's own backup is turned on (Google backup on Android, iCloud on iPhone), the operating system may back up the App's data to your account and bring it back when you move to a new phone. Google or Apple keeps that backup in your account; we cannot see or access it. On Android only your dhikr data is included. You can turn this off in your phone's backup settings.

3. Notifications

The App shows at most one reminder notification a day. Your phone's notification permission is requested once, after you complete your first dhikr, never when the App first opens. If you decline, it is not asked again on its own. You can turn the reminder off or change its time in Settings. Notifications are scheduled on the phone, not sent from a server. You can turn the permission off at any time in your phone's settings.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows ads through AdMob, a service of Google LLC. Ads never appear on the counting screen or in the wird list. Rewarded ads play only when you choose to watch one. Banner ads appear only on the home (Today), Goals, Vaham and dhikr-complete screens.

When showing ads, Google may collect and process: - the advertising ID (Android Advertising ID), - IP address and the approximate location derived from it (city/country level), - device and operating system information, - interactions with ads (impressions, clicks), - diagnostic and performance data (for example crash logs).

Google uses this information to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent to personalised ads through Google's consent form, and can change their choice in Settings. On iOS, the App does not ask for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency) and does not access your advertising identifier (IDFA).

Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences. How Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Donations (in-app purchases)

Payments in "Support the developer" are made through Google Play Billing or Apple's in-app purchase system. Google or Apple processes your payment details. We receive only confirmation that a purchase was completed. Donating gives you no extra features or privileges in the App.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Deleting your data

We hold no account or data about you. To delete the data on your phone, uninstall the App or clear its data in your phone's settings. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings > Google > Ads.

8. Your rights

Data protection laws (such as the GDPR in the EU and KVKK in Türkiye) give you rights to access, correct, delete and object. Because the App does not send your data to us, most requests are handled on your phone. For advertising data you can also contact Google. For questions, write to us at destekekibi4406@gmail.com.

9. Changes

If we update this policy, we will publish the new version on this page and change the effective date.

10. Contact
