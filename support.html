Vaha — Support

Having a problem or have a suggestion? Write to us: destekekibi4406@gmail.com Including your phone model and a short description of the problem helps us help you faster.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data? Will I lose it if I change phones? All your data is stored only on your phone. Before changing phones, create a backup file with Settings > Create backup and save it to Drive or send it to yourself. On the new phone, pick the file with Settings > Restore backup.

Reminders don't arrive. Make sure notifications are allowed for Vaha in your phone's settings. On some Android phones, battery saving can delay notifications. Removing Vaha from battery restrictions fixes this.

When does the day reset? Your daily wird resets at midnight (00:00).

Why are some scenes locked? The Vaha scene and all dhikr are always free. The other scenes open for 6 hours after watching a short ad. Ads never appear while you are counting.

I donated. Why do I still see ads? Donations are voluntary support and don't unlock privileges in the App. Thank you for your support.

I found an error in a verse or hadith. Please tell us which dhikr and which card it was on. We'll re-check the text against its source.

Which languages is the App available in? Turkish, English, Arabic, Indonesian, Malay, Urdu, French, Bosnian, German, Hindi, Bengali, Russian and Uzbek. You can change the language in Settings > Dil · Language · اللغة.
